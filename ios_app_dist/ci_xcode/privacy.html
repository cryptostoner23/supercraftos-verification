SuperCraftOS Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

SuperCraftOS does not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data. The app is a control surface for a self-hosted agent system that you run. Nothing in this app sends your information to the developer.

What the app stores on your device

  • Backend address — the address of your own SuperCraftOS control center, so the app can connect to it. Stored in the app's own preferences on your device.
  • Saved endpoints — the list of backend addresses you bookmark, also stored only on your device.

Both are removed when you delete the app. Neither is sent anywhere by the app itself.

What the app sends, and where

Everything the app sends goes only to the backend address you configured. That is a server you control. The app sends:

DataWhere it goes
Control session tokenYour backend, over HTTPS
Agent queries you startYour backend, over HTTPS
Approval decisions, grants, quests, product requestsYour backend, over HTTPS
Text you select for a keyboard actionYour backend, over HTTPS, or to the on-device model

The developer of this app has no access to that traffic and no access to your backend.

On-device model

The custom keyboard can rewrite, correct, or explain text using Apple's on-device language model, without sending it anywhere. That is the fallback used when your backend cannot be reached. Text processed on-device does not leave your device.

Third-party services

The app itself contacts no third-party analytics, advertising, or attribution service. Model providers, search providers, and any other service your agent uses are contacted by your backend, not by this app, and are configured by you.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Allow Full Access (keyboard only)Apple requires it for a keyboard that uses the network. It is needed to reach your backend. It is off until you turn it on.
NetworkTo reach your backend.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or health permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete. All data that exists lives on your own device and your own server.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date, and the app's App Store listing will point here.
